## Projects

### 1. Safeflow

A cross-chain bridge and streaming vault that enables users to bridge USDC from Ethereum Sepolia to Stacks Testnet. Once converted to USDCx, funds are locked in a Clarity smart contract and "dripped" to recipients based on Bitcoin block height.

- Live URL: https://safeflow-beta.vercel.app/

### 2. PiggyBag

PiggyBag is a student-focused fundraising platform built on Algorand. Creators launch fundraising projects, receive on-chain contributions, and issue project tokens with transparent, verifiable activity.

- Live URL: https://piggybag.vercel.app

### 3. Cantiffin

A web app built to skip canteen lines for universities. Reached over 70+ users and 10+ orders daily, but is currently halted due to tight canteen margins and high university regulations.

### 4. Truepaper

Truepaper is a platform to host degree certificates on the blockchain. This comes after massive fake degree scams and enormous time wasted in verifying degrees. The project has been approved for a testing phase by the director of the university. We plan to bring down degree verification time from 14 days to 14 seconds.
